# Awesome-Fitness-Club-Management

## Top Fitness Club Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Gym & Studio Management, Memberships, Class Booking, Billing & Member Experience*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Fitness Club Management**. These systems help gyms, boutique studios, CrossFit boxes, and clubs manage memberships, class schedules, payments, access, and member engagement.



**Examples** include Mindbody, ABC Fitness, Wodify, Zen Planner, Glofox, PushPress, TeamUp, Pike13, PerfectGym, and ClubReady (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full commercial gym-management suites dominate the market. Practical open options include **wger**, **GYM-One**, and community gym CRM projects. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Mindbody](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/)**  

  Leading wellness and fitness business platform for scheduling, memberships, payments, and marketing across gyms, studios, and spas.



- **[ABC Fitness](https://www.abcfitness.com/)**  

  Enterprise fitness management software used by large clubs and chains for memberships, billing, club operations, and member engagement.



- **[Wodify](https://www.wodify.com/)**  

  Performance and gym management platform popular with CrossFit and functional fitness affiliates for WODs, tracking, and member operations.



- **[Zen Planner](https://zenplanner.com/)**  

  Gym and studio management software focused on memberships, scheduling, billing, and member retention for fitness businesses.



- **[Glofox (ABC Glofox)](https://www.glofox.com/)**  

  Boutique fitness and studio management platform for class booking, memberships, payments, and branded member apps.



- **[PushPress](https://www.pushpress.com/)**  

  Gym management software oriented toward CrossFit and functional fitness boxes with memberships, billing, and community features.



- **[TeamUp](https://goteamup.com/)**  

  Class booking and studio management platform for fitness and wellness businesses with scheduling, payments, and member tools.



- **[Pike13](https://www.pike13.com/)**  

  Business management platform for fitness and wellness studios covering scheduling, clients, and payments.



- **[PerfectGym](https://www.perfectgym.com/)**  

  Club management system for fitness chains and multi-site operators with memberships, access control, and operations tools.



- **[ClubReady](https://www.clubready.com/)**  

  Fitness club management software for memberships, billing, sales, and club operations.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **wger** for workout, nutrition, and light multi-user gym tracking with full data ownership.

- Deploying **GYM-One** or similar open gym CRM projects for membership and class basics.

- Extending **Odoo/ERPNext** when the club needs broader ERP capabilities alongside memberships.

- Accepting that polished member apps, integrated billing at scale, access hardware, and multi-site operations still favor commercial platforms (Mindbody, ABC Fitness, Glofox, Wodify, PerfectGym, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, avoiding marketplace lock-in, and lower cost for independent gyms and boxes.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage members and memberships in an open CRM or ERP → schedule classes with open calendar tools → track workouts in wger or similar → handle payments via open or commercial gateways → add check-in/access as needed. Suitable for small studios and technically capable operators. Most established clubs and chains rely on commercial fitness management platforms for reliability and integrations.
